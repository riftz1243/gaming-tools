About GameTools

PC gaming information, kept simple.

GameTools is a small project built around a simple idea: common PC gaming questions should not require digging through dozens of pages just to find a useful starting point.

01

What is GameTools?

GameTools is a collection of free browser-based tools and guides for PC gamers.

The site focuses on practical tasks such as choosing graphics settings, estimating game download times and finding a game to play.

Settings Finder Get a graphics settings starting point based on your hardware and preferences.
Game Guides More detailed settings recommendations for specific games and GPUs.
Simple Utilities Small gaming tools designed to give you an answer quickly.
02

How settings recommendations work

The Settings Finder compares broad GPU performance, CPU capability, game demand, resolution and the type of experience you choose.

It then creates a practical starting point for settings such as textures, shadows, reflections, upscaling and ray tracing.

These recommendations are not presented as measured FPS benchmarks. There are too many differences between PCs, drivers, game versions and individual scenes to guarantee a particular frame rate from a general recommendation.

03

Why no fake FPS numbers?

It can be tempting to say that a particular graphics card will get an exact frame rate at certain settings, but an exact number should come from an actual controlled benchmark.

GameTools instead uses terms such as Limited, Moderate, Good and Strong to describe expected graphics headroom. Dedicated guides provide more specific settings to try without pretending they are laboratory benchmark results.

04

A growing project

GameTools is still growing. More games, hardware options, settings guides and useful gaming tools can be added over time.

The goal is to keep the site lightweight and straightforward as it expands rather than filling every page with unnecessary content.
