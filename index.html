What is GameTools?

GameTools is a growing collection of free tools and guides for PC gamers. The goal is to make common gaming decisions simpler, from choosing graphics settings to estimating download times.

Settings recommendations are starting points rather than guaranteed benchmark results. Actual performance can vary depending on hardware, drivers, game updates and system configuration.
