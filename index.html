About GameTools

Straightforward gaming tools without the clutter.

GameTools is a small collection of utilities and guides built for PC gamers. The goal is to provide useful answers quickly without forcing users through long articles, accounts or unnecessary menus.

Graphics recommendations are starting points rather than guaranteed benchmark results. Performance can vary depending on hardware, game updates, drivers and system configuration.
