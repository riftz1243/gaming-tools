Should you use Ray Tracing?

Ray tracing can significantly increase GPU load in Cyberpunk 2077. If smooth performance is your priority, start with ray tracing disabled.

If you have performance headroom, enable ray tracing gradually and test demanding areas of Night City before increasing it further.

DLSS and Frame Generation

At 1440p, DLSS Quality is a good starting point because it can improve performance while maintaining strong image quality.

Frame Generation can increase displayed frame rates in supported configurations, but it should not be treated as a replacement for a good base frame rate.

Important: performance varies

These settings are recommended starting points rather than guaranteed benchmark results. Your actual performance can change depending on CPU, RAM, drivers, game updates and other system settings.

Test the settings on your own PC and adjust the most demanding options until you reach the balance of graphics quality and performance you prefer.
